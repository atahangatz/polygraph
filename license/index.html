01The short version

  • Every edition's code is GPL-3.0-or-later. You can use it for any work, paid or not, read it and change it.
  • Buying Pro or Studio pays for the build, every 1.x and 2.x update, and support by email.
  • Pro is for one person, on as many of their own computers as they like. Studio is for a team and is counted by person.
  • Anything you make with Polygraph is yours.
  • The GPL covers the code, not the name Polygraph or its logo.
  • Refunds have their own page.

02The code is GPL

Polygraph's code is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later, in every edition: Free, Pro and Studio. Add-ons run inside Blender, which is itself GPL, so Blender add-ons have to be.

Among other things, the GPL lets you:

  • run Polygraph for any purpose, paid work included;
  • read and change the code (the zip is the source, since it's all Python);
  • pass copies on, changed or not, as long as you pass them on under the GPL too.

The GPL itself has the full terms. Where this page and the GPL seem to disagree about the code, the GPL wins.

Passing it on

The GPL allows you to share the paid builds, and we won't pretend otherwise. There's no key, no account and no check inside Polygraph. We'd only ask this: if Polygraph saves you time every week, buy your own copy. Updates and support come with a purchase, not with the zip, and sales are what keep Polygraph maintained.

03What you're buying

A Pro or Studio purchase gets you:

The build
The zip for your edition, ready to install, with everything that edition lists. A cheaper edition carries no locked code from a dearer one: what it doesn't have isn't in its zip.
Updates
Every 1.x and 2.x release of your edition, from the store you bought it from. See Updates and 2.0.
Support
Answers by email from the people who make Polygraph. See Support.

Polygraph is made by Waddle by Duck Initiative, called "we" on this page. You buy it through Gumroad or Superhive, which take the payment, charge any tax that applies and send your receipt or invoice. Their terms of sale apply as well as these.

Polygraph Free costs nothing; on Gumroad you can pay what you like for it. The GPL and the sections here about commercial use, the name, the warranty and changes apply to Free too. The parts about seats, updates and email support are for the paid editions.

04Seats

Pro
One person, on every computer they work on: a desktop, a laptop, the machine at home. It isn't meant to be shared between people.
Studio
A team. The order covers five people. For more, add extra seats on Gumroad or pick the ten-seat variant on Superhive. Larger teams can write to support@duckinitiative.com.

A Studio seat is a person who uses Polygraph in their work, not a computer. A few cases that come up:

  • Build servers and render farms running the command line check don't need seats, because nobody sits at them.
  • A contractor who uses your Studio build takes one of your seats while they work with you. One who brings their own Pro doesn't.
  • When someone leaves, their seat passes to whoever takes over. A Studio licence belongs to the company that bought it, not to one person.

Seats aren't enforced by a key, and the GPL wouldn't let us make them a condition of running the code. They're the size of your order: how many people it pays for, and how many get updates and support from us under it. We don't audit anyone. If your team grows past its seats, add seats.

05Commercial use and your work

Any edition, Free included, can be used for commercial work: client jobs, games and apps you sell, assets you put up on a store. Studio isn't a commercial licence. It's what a team needs: seats, the build server check, shared budgets, your name on reports and faster answers.

Anything you make with Polygraph is yours. We claim nothing in your models, scenes, reports, exports or budget profiles. Reports say they were written by Polygraph, and you can send them to anyone.

The budgets that ship with it

Shipped budgets take their limits from each platform's public documentation, and each one cites its page and the date it was checked. Platforms change their limits, so check the source before you ship anything that depends on one. Meta Quest, VRChat, Roblox, Fortnite, visionOS and the other platform names belong to their owners, none of whom make, endorse or sponsor Polygraph.

06Updates and 2.0

  • Every 1.x and 2.x release of your edition is included at no extra cost.
  • 2.0 is a free upgrade for everyone who bought 1.x. The price for new buyers goes up when 2.0 comes out. What you paid stays what you paid.
  • Updates arrive where you bought: your Gumroad library or your Superhive account. Installing one keeps your settings and your profiles.
  • If there's ever a 3.0, it may be a paid upgrade. If it is, 2.x owners will get it for less, and we'll say so well before it ships.
  • What you've downloaded keeps working. There's no licence key, no account and no expiry date.

Each release is tested on Windows, macOS and Linux, on every Blender version from 4.2 to 5.3. While a version is the one we sell, we keep it working with each new Blender release. When Blender changes in a way an add-on can't follow straight away, the Changes section of the guide says so.

08The name, the logo and the rest

The GPL covers the code. It doesn't cover the things that tell people Polygraph is ours:

  • The name Polygraph, as the name of a Blender add-on, and the Polygraph logo. Section 7(e) of the GPL lets us keep trademark rights out of the licence, and we do.
  • This website, the guide's text, and the store images and videos, which are © Waddle by Duck Initiative.

You're welcome to use the name, the logo and screenshots to talk about Polygraph: in reviews, tutorials, videos and articles, or to say your own tool works with it. What you can't do is use them as the name or logo of your own product or build, or in a way that suggests we made or endorse something we didn't.

Changed versions

You may change Polygraph and share your version under the GPL. Give it a different name and an icon of its own, so nobody mistakes it for ours or asks us to support it.

Inside the zip

Everything in the zip is GPL-3.0-or-later, the icons and the shipped budgets included. Some icons are drawn after Blender's own, which are by the Blender Foundation and its contributors under CC BY-SA 4.0. Its Windows build carries Microsoft's C++ runtime, which Microsoft lets programs redistribute.

09No warranty, and our limit

As sections 15 and 16 of the GPL say, Polygraph comes with no warranty. We test every release, but a count is advice, not a certificate. Check anything critical in the engine itself before you ship.

As far as the law allows, we aren't responsible for indirect losses, such as lost work, missed deadlines or lost income, and the most we're responsible for on any purchase is what you paid for it. None of this takes away rights you have as a consumer that the law doesn't let anyone sign away.
