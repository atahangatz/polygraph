Refunds

01The short version

Write to us within 30 days of buying, and you get a full refund when:

  • Polygraph doesn't work on your computer or your Blender, and we can't fix it within a week.
  • It doesn't do something its store page or the guide says it does.
  • You bought by mistake: the wrong edition, the same thing twice, or more seats than you meant to.

Not sure yet? Try Polygraph Free first. It has the same engine counts, mesh checks and report as the paid editions, so you can see Polygraph run on your setup before you pay.

02Wrong edition, or bought twice

Bought Pro and meant Studio, or the other way round? Buy the one you meant, send us both order numbers, and we'll refund the first. A duplicate order is refunded in full, and so are Studio seats you didn't mean to add.

03What isn't covered

  • Requests made more than 30 days after the purchase.
  • A sale or discount that starts after you bought.
  • Not knowing how to use it. Write to us instead and we'll walk you through it.
  • Things Polygraph never claimed to do. It doesn't decimate or retopologise your meshes, and it doesn't run inside Unity or Unreal. The store pages say so.
  • Deciding you don't need it after all, when it works as described. That's what Free is for.

04How to ask

  1. Write to support@duckinitiative.com. If you bought on Superhive, message us from the product page there instead, so Superhive's team can see the conversation too.
  2. Include your order number, your Blender version and your system, and a screenshot of the Polygraph tab if something's wrong.
  3. We answer within three days. If it's something we can fix, we'll try first. Often it's an install step, like Safari unzipping the download.
  4. If we can't fix it within a week of your message, or it's one of the other cases above, we refund you in full through the store, to the card or account you paid with.

You don't have to wait past that week for a fix that might come later.

05After a refund

A refund cancels the order. Updates and support for it stop, and the store takes the download out of your library. We won't ask you to prove you've deleted anything. How long the money takes to reach you depends on the store and your bank.

06Your rights, and the stores' rules

Consumer law where you live may give you more than this page does, and nothing here takes that away.

Gumroad and Superhive have refund rules of their own, and either can step in. On Superhive, if we turn a request down and you think it qualifies, you can ask Superhive's team to look at it, and their decision stands.

Please write to us before you open a dispute with your bank or card company. A refund is quicker for you and simpler for everyone.

07Polygraph Free

Free costs nothing. If you chose to pay for it on Gumroad and would like that back, ask within 30 days and you'll get it, no questions asked.

08Contact

support@duckinitiative.com. Every message gets an answer. What a purchase covers, seats and updates are on the Licence page.
